### What's new

- Hide the language direction for the configured default pair, leaving subtitles for additional meanings. Reverse lookups and other language pairs still show their direction.
- Keep lookup results in the configured provider order, including cached and background results.
- Fix iCIBA Quick Look controls and online pronunciation playback.

### Requirements and installation

Apple silicon Mac, Alfred 5.5 or newer with Powerpack, and internet access for web lookups. Optional Apple Translation requires macOS 26 and installed language models.

Download the `.alfredworkflow` asset below and open it with Alfred.

### 更新内容

- 默认语言方向不再重复显示在副标题中，为更多释义留出空间；反向查询及其他语言组合仍显示翻译方向。
- 查询结果始终按配置的词典顺序排列，包括缓存结果和后台查询结果。
- 修复 iCIBA 快速预览中的控件及在线发音播放。

### 使用要求与安装

需要 Apple 芯片 Mac、Alfred 5.5 或更新版本及 Powerpack；网页查词需要联网。可选的 Apple 翻译需要 macOS 26 和已安装的语言模型。

下载下方的 `.alfredworkflow` 文件，使用 Alfred 打开即可安装。
