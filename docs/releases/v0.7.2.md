## English

### What's new

- Review saved words in daily sessions, including reverse recall and typed answers.
- Search saved vocabulary and see when reviews are due.
- Set a daily review reminder and pronounce words during review.
- Back up and restore saved words as JSON, or export them as CSV.
- Faster review grading and refined WordPing menus.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac, Alfred Powerpack, and your own Youdao AI Cloud app key and secret.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- 每天分批复习已保存的单词，支持反向回忆和输入答案。
- 搜索生词，并查看何时需要复习。
- 设置每日复习提醒，在复习时听单词发音。
- 将生词备份或恢复为 JSON 文件，也可以导出为 CSV 文件。
- 加快复习评分操作，并改进 WordPing 菜单。

需要 Apple 芯片 Mac、Alfred Powerpack，以及自行申请的有道智云应用密钥（app key 和 secret）。
